For technical readers

The release is rolling out to Free and Go users in the U.S. on web, iOS, and Android; Plus and Pro are also listed as available in the current Help Center guide. Bank data connects through Plaid and credit-report data through Experian as separate connections. Data coverage depends on supported institutions, sync dates, and what the provider shares.

Who should pay attention?

U.S. users who want a consolidated view of personal finances, and product teams assessing how assistants use connected sensitive data.

Availability and limitations: U.S. availability only; rollout may take time to appear. ChatGPT cannot move money, pay bills, change account settings, trade, or file taxes, and it is not a financial, tax, legal, or investment adviser. Use synthetic examples in this walkthrough; never enter account credentials or real financial details here.

For technical readers

The API model ID is gpt-6.1-sol. Standard API pricing per 1M tokens is $2 input, $0.10 cached input, $2.50 cache writes, and $10 output for prompts up to 272K input tokens. Above 272K input tokens, OpenAI lists higher rates. ChatGPT availability is in Work and Codex, beginning with Pro and expanding to Plus, Business, Enterprise, and Edu; it is not available in regular Chat. Enterprise and Edu owners can control access.

Who should pay attention?

Developers and teams with repeatable complex tasks, and technical buyers comparing model quality, latency, and total API cost.

Availability and limitations: ChatGPT Work and Codex rollout depends on plan and workspace permissions; API usage is metered and rate limits vary by tier. OpenAI’s performance descriptions are vendor claims. Run a representative evaluation before switching.

For technical readers

Dots are powered by GPT-6 Astra and can use connected apps with permissions defined by the user. They are gradually rolling out to eligible Pro and Business Premium users aged 18+ in supported markets. Pro access excludes the EEA, Switzerland, and UK at launch. Enterprise is a beta, off by default. OpenAI says first-month usage will not count toward eligible plans’ allowances; later usage terms will be announced.

Who should pay attention?

Eligible users with repeatable, bounded tasks and teams assessing delegated work, permissions, approvals, and ongoing monitoring.

Availability and limitations: Gradual access is desktop-first and market/plan restricted; enterprise admins must enable the beta. Limit app permissions, avoid unsupervised consequential actions, and review returned work. An always-on agent can make mistakes.

  3. 03

Ready when you are. This is a local learning simulation. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

For technical readers

At launch, Pro 500 subscriptions are available on ChatGPT web. Astra Ultrafast is included only with Pro 500 and uses the plan allowance before the credit balance. Buying credits on Pro 100 or Pro 200 does not unlock Ultrafast. Pro 200 is again available for new subscriptions at $200/month; affected users receive details about allowance changes by email, while the monthly price remains the same.

Who should pay attention?

Organizations and professionals who can quantify frequent premium-model use and the time value of faster output.

Availability and limitations: High recurring price; web-only purchase at launch; plan terms and allowance usage apply. Estimate actual usage and compare with alternatives before procurement.

For technical readers

Pages can start from a conversation, template, or blank page; users can edit directly or ask ChatGPT to revise text, add charts, or create interactive content. Space brings Pages, files, and related work together for eligible Pro, Business, and Enterprise users on desktop and web. Site owners and editors on Plus and Pro can use Edit site in a desktop browser, review changes, and publish when ready. Owners can create recurring cloud schedules in Automations where Sites is supported.

Who should pay attention?

Writers, educators, marketers, and small teams creating explainers, reports, and lightweight sites together.

Availability and limitations: Page sharing exposes the content added to that Page to people who can view it; it does not grant access to private chats or memory. Space and Site features depend on plan and availability. Enterprise Space sharing is an opt-in preview.
