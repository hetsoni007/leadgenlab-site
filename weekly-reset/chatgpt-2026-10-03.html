ChatGPT gets more practical.

This week’s product update brings shopping try-on and a faster way to turn paper pages into a PDF. Learn the plain-language version, the system view, and a safe way to try each feature.

New to ChatGPT? Read the beginner guide before trying the new features.

What happened · 1 October

Two small workflows make more of ChatGPT’s camera and image features.

For clothing and accessories, eligible product listings can offer virtual try-on using a selfie, and users can save finds in Favorites or folders. On iOS, a camera Scan option can capture multiple pages and combine them into a PDF for a chat.

In everyday language

You can preview how an item might look on you, keep a shortlist of products, or take photos of paper notes and ask ChatGPT to work with the pages as one document.

For technical readers

The try-on flow uses an uploaded or captured reference image and generated product visualization; OpenAI says the reference photo is retained for future try-ons unless changed or deleted in Personalization settings. The scan flow captures multi-page input and packages it as a PDF for the conversation. Availability differs by platform and rollout.

Privacy check: use an image you’re comfortable storing for future try-ons, learn where the reference photo can be removed, and avoid capturing private or confidential documents unless your account and organization settings allow it.

Who should pay attention?

Shoppers can explore try-on and saved finds. Students, educators, and office teams can test multi-page scanning on non-sensitive notes. For work documents, check your organization’s data policies before uploading.

Also announced this week

On 29 September, OpenAI described Pro 500, a $500/month web subscription with access to Astra Ultrafast in ChatGPT Work and Codex. It is a premium plan detail for eligible high-usage workflows—not a default recommendation for general readers.
