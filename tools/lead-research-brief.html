BEFORE ANY OUTREACH
  • Open the source yourself and confirm the detail, date, and context.
  • Decide whether the role and problem connection are genuinely relevant.
  • Check contact accuracy, permissions, and applicable outreach rules.
  • Edit the draft in your own voice. Do not present an inference as a fact.

No input is stored when you leave this page.

A REASON, NOT A SHORTCUT

Signals invite a check.
They do not prove intent.

A public announcement can give you a relevant starting point. It cannot tell you what a particular person wants, whether they own the problem, or whether now is the right time. Make that judgment with evidence and care.
