Reader guide · AI tools

Choose an AI assistant for the work you actually do.

Compare assistants by the tasks you need, the information you share, and the quality of their results—not by a feature list alone.

Editorial note: This is a criteria-first guide, not a paid ranking. Product-specific comparisons should be reviewed and dated before publication.

Five questions to ask

01

Does it do your task well?

Test the same real-world prompt in each tool and compare how much correction you need to make.

02

Can you verify the answer?

For research, look for sources you can inspect. For important decisions, verify claims independently.

03

What data will you share?

Understand storage, retention, training use, permissions, and how to remove connected data.

04

What does it really cost?

Review limits, tiers, and the price after a trial. Consider the total cost for your actual use.

05

Does it fit your routine?

Check language support, accessibility, device compatibility, integrations, and export options.

✓

Try it with low-stakes work

Give it a short trial with non-sensitive tasks before deciding whether it earns a place in your workflow.
