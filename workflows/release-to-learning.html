A LeadGenLab field note · Learn by walking it through

Turn one AI release into a 30-minute team learning sprint

Help technical and non-technical teammates move from a vendor announcement to a shared, low-risk experiment with a clear decision at the end.

Het Soni · LeadGenLabInteractive guide · 6 minute read

Teams often receive release news as a link with no time to understand it. A short learning sprint turns one meaningful update into shared context and a practical next step.

Minutes 0–5: verify what changed

Read the vendor’s primary source. Identify whether the item is a model, feature, pricing, access, or policy update. Separate statements from evidence and note any rollout limits.

Minutes 5–10: explain it at two depths

Write one plain-language sentence for colleagues who only need the outcome. Add a technical note for those who will evaluate access, integration, cost, latency, or data handling. Let each reader choose their level.

Minutes 10–20: pick one role-based task

Choose a familiar workflow for sales, marketing, operations, or development. Use synthetic or approved information. Set a success measure before anyone compares results.

Minutes 20–27: walk through the example

Have one person run the task, one person check the output, and one person record where the feature helped or fell short. Treat the assistant’s answer as a draft until verified.

Minutes 27–30: choose the next step

Write down what you learned and choose: test again, adopt in a narrow workflow, or leave it alone. A small decision with a named owner keeps curiosity from becoming an unreviewed rollout.
