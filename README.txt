LeadGenLab is a practical research desk for founders and lean revenue teams: start with a public company signal, test its connection to a real problem, and review before any outreach. Existing guides, workflows, sourced AI updates, citations, and editorial caveats remain part of the site. The local Lead Research Brief tool assembles a draft in the browser and offers copy and plain-text download actions.

The brief tool makes no AI/API request, does not fetch or verify entered URLs, and does not save or send its inputs. Its content is not included in analytics; it is a drafting aid only. The contact form opens an email draft addressed to Het and provides a copy fallback. Nothing is sent until the visitor chooses Send. Release sources are linked to official vendor pages. The update desk is manually maintained; a daily review schedule can prepare local changes but cannot publish these files to a live domain.

Published for LeadGenLab by Soni Consultancy Services. Contact: het.soni@soniconsultancyservices.com

Visual system: warm field-note paper, forest green, measured lime, serif display typography, high-contrast text, visible keyboard focus, skip-to-content links, larger article typography, and topic-specific visual examples throughout articles and release briefings.
