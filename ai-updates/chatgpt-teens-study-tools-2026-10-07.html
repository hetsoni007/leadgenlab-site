AI Release Desk · 7 October 2026 · OPENAI · CHATGPT FOR TEENS

ChatGPT adds more study tools for teens and a planned College Planner.

OpenAI announced photo capture for multiple pages of notes, flashcards, interactive quizzes, and a College Planner it says is coming soon for teens in the United States.

What changed · plain-language and technical view

ChatGPT adds more study tools for teens and a planned College Planner.

Students can turn class notes into study materials and revisit them later. The planned College Planner will organize application tasks and deadlines; students still need to make their own choices and check school requirements.

For technical readers

OpenAI says continuous multi-photo capture combines pages into one PDF and is currently available on iOS, with Android support in development. Flashcards and quizzes can be created from uploaded notes or a topic; saved decks can be revisited and scheduled. College Planner is planned initially for U.S. high-school students in grades 10–12 applying to four-year colleges.

Who should pay attention?

Teens, families, educators, and advisers supporting study habits or college applications.

Availability and limitations: College Planner is announced as a coming feature, not a generally available launch; the initial scope is U.S. grades 10–12 and four-year colleges. Multi-page capture is on iOS while Android is still in progress. Verify deadlines, aid, and requirements with each school and an adviser.
