AI Release Desk · 7 October 2026 · OPENAI · CHATGPT MODEL + PRODUCT

GPT‑6 brings interactive visual responses to ChatGPT.

GPT‑6 can combine text with diagrams, buttons, charts, forms, and small interactive experiences. OpenAI says it can also begin answering sooner while continuing to reason.

Official date 7 October 2026 · 5 minute read · Verified against the primary vendor source

What changed · plain-language and technical view

GPT‑6 brings interactive visual responses to ChatGPT.

Instead of explaining everything in paragraphs, ChatGPT may show a visual you can tap or change. Ask for an explanation, explore the parts, then check the important details before using the result.

For technical readers

OpenAI describes a native component library and a compiler that streams interface elements as the model generates them. The model chooses between prose, visuals, and interaction based on the prompt. Its progressive response behavior interleaves partial answers with continued reasoning; published speed and quality comparisons are OpenAI evaluations.

Who should pay attention?

Anyone learning a complex topic, planning a task, or prototyping a lightweight tool inside ChatGPT; especially educators, analysts, and product teams.

Availability and limitations: The global ChatGPT rollout began with Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise on 7 October and expanded to Free and Go from 8 October. Rollout may take time; Enterprise availability depends on admin settings. This change is for the Chat tab; Work and Codex models were not changed by this release.
